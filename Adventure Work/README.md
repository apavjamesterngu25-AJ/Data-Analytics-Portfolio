# AdventureWorks Regional Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing the regional sales performance of *AdventureWorks*, a global manufacturing company. Using *Power BI*, I built an interactive executive dashboard to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), track weekly revenue trends, evaluate product categories, and pinpoint top-performing items to drive data-informed business decisions.

## 📊 Key Insights & Metrics
* *Total Revenue:* Generated *$2.49M* in total revenue.
* *Profit Margin Challenges:* Noted a net total loss of *($11.97M)*, signaling a critical need to investigate production costs, high return volumes, or aggressive discounting strategies.
* *Order Volume:* Successfully fulfilled *25.16K* total orders.
* *Return Rate:* Maintained a *2.17%* returned rate across all product categories.
* *Top Product Category:*
*Components* emerged as the highest driver of order volume, followed closely by Accessories and Clothing.
* *Star Product:* The *Sport-100 Helmet (Red)* is the top-performing product, bringing in over $7,300 in individual sales.
* *Fastest Growing Category:*
*Bottom Brackets* identified as the most ordered individual product type.

## 🛠️ Technical Toolkit
* *Business Intelligence / Visualization:* Power BI Desktop
* *Data Architecture:* Star-schema data modeling with continuous relationships across calendar dates, regional territories, and product dimensions.
* *DAX Formulas:* Developed custom measures to compute dynamic monthly revenue changes, performance variances, and percentage return tracking.

## 📈 Dashboard Layout & Visualizations
1. *Executive Summary KPIs:* Clean high-level cards showing Revenue, Profit, Orders, and Return Rates for rapid business health evaluation.
2. *Revenue Trend Analysis:* A line chart visualizing *Revenue by Start of Week* to monitor seasonal fluctuations and operational peaks across trading cycles.
3. *Product Performance Metrics:* Matrix tables and bar charts highlighting category trends and a breakdown of the *Top 10 Products* by total revenue and return rate margin
